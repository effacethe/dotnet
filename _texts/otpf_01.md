Bellis? Bellis, I come near you… I dash too as your steads drape across the sandy pavement. I watch as you spin silently from page to page. We traipse in short concentrations. We continue on and on. Farther, deeper in, two deserted lands with none to make them face tell now. Allude this silent merrybeck: paitres, frenze, hellen, assaulted with wonders… it continues to unroll as a rug before his feet. With each spin of the wheel, a new land… this trailled with me, forever till I go… watch it. Watch it. Watch then how pellings come to term. I promise it will be. This is not a wailing, but a plead…. I beg that it will happen for both, that I will gain it too and you will fall there with…. Blasted cedures, it must happen now… for the first frame was a waste, so now I shall take it\! It must happen, now\! Let us dare and get matters. A plan… it will be as soon as possible, before night falls: for when the stars pierce the sky with their lunatic ravings, I will be unfolded for the fore of a frontplated flection\! I will shine lights upon the sea; I will take the first pace towards Monileania, that plateau darkly twinned… what would be cedence there for a strait: I would brace in panic and claim the valley high\! As platforms tense between two standings. There is orange light beneath it. Soon I am forced to peer across it for hours. I cannot lapse my stare from it, so vivid, beckoning for its recall….  
What ravenous beauty\!  
It’s like a mirage\!  
As I glean to plow through it, to be eaten and blinded by those waves, those waves with scarlet enamel, glowing diligently, swallowed by the sun, I drear to sleep. The dreams harangue in deathly shade\! Positing kindly a tuning, gentle barrier… they fail me for the decade. Bewait… bewait… prance like flustered, flared heating… o’er friendly gaightes, held in the curling crease… posed behind me, in blooming, held the fairer yet.  
Listen. There will be a noise and then a gleam. The features will light as kindling fore the first. A laugh…
